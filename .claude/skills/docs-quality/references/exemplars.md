# Pages to compare against

Compare a page to the one doing its job, not to an average of the site.

## Tutorial step — `tutorial/step3.mdx`

The density target for a step. What it does well:

- Opens on the previous step's dead end in two lines, then the command.
- Names its change as two fields and says the rules did not move.
- Spends its middle on one finding — that a recorded decision is a sight paired
  with an action, and why a board history could not replace it.
- Every claim in 確かめ方 names the test that fixes it.
- まだないもの says what the guest side does not record and hands off.

## Measurement-led step — `tutorial/step5.mdx`

The target when a page's content is a result rather than a feature. It reports
a twelve-run grid, states the negative result plainly, and separates "this is a
limit of solved games" from "this is a limit of the technique" — the distinction
the reader would otherwise draw wrongly.

Note what it does not do: it does not bury the finding under the setup.

## Reference — `integration/stage-rule-set.mdx`

The target for a configuration or interface surface. Table-forward, exhaustive
for the thing it covers, with a reason line under each group. Do not rewrite
pages like this into narrative.

## Glossary — `architecture/terms.mdx`

The target for a lookup page. Prose walks the pipeline in order so the reader
gets the *because*, and a compact table at the end serves the reader who came
to look one word up. Both, not either.
